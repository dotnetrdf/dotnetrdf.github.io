A bug-fix and minor enhancement update of the dotNetRDF library is now available for download. 

This release fixes a couple of bugs reported by community members as well as performance enhancements for the pull-processing SPARQL query engine.

For full details, please refer to the [Change Log](https://github.com/dotnetrdf/dotnetrdf/blob/master/ChangeLog.txt).

dotNetRDF 3.4.1 can be found [here on NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/packages/dotNetRDF/3.4.1) and also on [GitHub](https://github.com/dotnetrdf/dotnetrdf/releases/tag/v3.4.1).
